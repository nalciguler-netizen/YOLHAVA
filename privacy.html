Privacy Policy

YolHava mobile app • Last updated: 6 October 2026 (version 2.3)

In short: YolHava has no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics tools. Your saved trips and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to a server of ours. To show route, weather and road information, the coordinates you choose are sent to the map and weather services listed below.

2. Data we process and where it is stored

2.1 Stored only on your phone

  • Saved trips (start and destination, days, departure time, preferred route)
  • Recently used addresses and app settings (language, units, map style, voice, layers)
  • Your own TomTom API key, if you enter one
  • A temporary cache of stations along a route (up to 7 days)
  • Offline trip packages you download: route, directions, weather forecast, road events at that time, stations and the map tiles around the route
  • An in-app error log (memory only; cleared when the app closes)

2.2 Location

  • Your location is used only with your permission and only while the app is open. The app does not collect location in the background.
  • It is used to show where you are, to start a route from your location, and in driving mode to show your progress, speed and the speed limit. Your speed is processed only on your phone and is never sent or stored.
  • Your location history is not recorded.

2.4 Accident and road reports

If you use “Report incident”, the report's category (e.g. accident, roadworks), location, direction of travel, time and a random device number generated by the app for this phone are stored on our server. No name, phone number or account details are collected. The category, location and time are shown to other users; the device number is not shown and is used only to prevent abuse (too many reports, repeated votes). Your “still there?” answers are stored with the same device number. Reports disappear within a few hours depending on their category and are fully deleted after 1 day at the latest. The server runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure.

2.5 Feedback

The “Send feedback” button opens WhatsApp and you choose the recipient. Unless you untick it, the message includes the app version, phone and operating system details, the names of your last route's start and destination, and recent error entries. Your location is not included. WhatsApp's own terms apply to the message.

2.6 Calendar reminders and voice guidance

Reminders create a calendar file on your phone or open Google Calendar; nothing is sent to us. Voice guidance uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

2.7 What we do not collect

  • No name, phone number or email address (unless you share it voluntarily in feedback).
  • No cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No special categories of personal data.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Location and coordinate data are processed to provide the route, weather, road event and station information you request. The legal basis is the performance of the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); in Turkey, KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)). Location access additionally depends on your consent through your phone's permission screen, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone settings.

4. International transfers

Because some of the services above are located abroad, using them transfers the relevant coordinates and your IP address to servers in other countries. This happens only during an action you start in the app (for example creating a route or searching for an address) and only to carry out that action.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data stored on your phone stays there until you delete it.
  • Settings → Legal → Delete all my data removes all saved trips, offline packages, settings and cache in one tap. Uninstalling the app or clearing its browser data has the same effect.
  • Our server only keeps the accident and road reports you submit and your confirmation votes; they are deleted automatically within 1 day. Contact us if you want a report removed earlier.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the EU, your local supervisory authority; in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority). Since your data is stored on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For any request, contact yolhava.destek@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

7. Children

YolHava is a driving and trip planning app. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security

All connections between the app and the services use encryption (HTTPS). The security of data stored on your phone depends on your device's lock screen and security settings.

9. Changes

When new features are added (for example a notification server, accounts or a paid plan), this policy will be updated and published on this page. Important changes are also announced in the app.
