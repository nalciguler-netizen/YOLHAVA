2. Nature of the service

  • YolHava is a free, ad-free app that provides weather forecasts, accident and roadworks reports, fuel and charging stations and voice guidance for your chosen route.
  • This information comes in real time from third-party services (see section 6). The developer does not produce this data and cannot guarantee its accuracy, timeliness or completeness.
  • Weather forecasts are uncertain by nature; fog, showers and snow in particular can form and clear quickly. “Snow cover” is a model estimate for the ground in the area and does not measure the road surface.
  • Speed limits come from TomTom data. Where no data is available, limits are estimated only within Turkey from general national limits and shown with a “~”. The posted road signs are always the valid speed limit. The app does not show or warn about the location of speed cameras.
  • Offline packages contain the information available at the time of download; accident and roadworks information may have changed since. A new route cannot be calculated without internet.
  • Directions are based on map data; new roads, temporary traffic arrangements, turn restrictions or closures may not be reflected.
  • Accident and roadworks information may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and not every event may be reported.
  • Station information comes from a community-built map; closed stations may appear and new ones may be missing. Check opening status, fuel types and charging connector compatibility yourself.
